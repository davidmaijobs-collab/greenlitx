Submission Agreement — GreenlitX

Last updated: [Date to be inserted on adoption, after legal review] Status: Draft for legal review. Do not publish as a final agreement until a qualified attorney has reviewed and approved this document.

This Submission Agreement applies when you submit material to GreenlitX. Part A covers original film projects submitted for possible publication. Part B covers completed films submitted for the Watch Free library. The general provisions in Part C apply to both.

Part A — Original film project submissions

A1. You retain your intellectual property

You retain all rights in your submitted project — including copyright and all rights in its title, story, characters, script, artwork, and other materials — whether or not the project is reviewed, published, supported, or produced.

GreenlitX claims no ownership of a submitted project, in whole or in part, under this Agreement.

The only rights GreenlitX receives are the limited licenses expressly stated in Section A2. No other rights are granted by implication.

A2. The limited license you grant

By submitting a project, you grant GreenlitX a limited, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to:

  • Store, copy for internal review systems, and review your submission internally; and
  • If — and only if — your project is approved for publication: display the materials you submitted for publication (such as the title, logline, synopsis, poster, and pitch materials) on the GreenlitX platform for as long as the project remains published.

This license does not give GreenlitX the right to produce your project, sell it, assign it, sublicense it for production, or use your materials for an unrelated project.

If you withdraw your project under Section A6, the publication license ends and GreenlitX will remove the project from public view as described there. Copies already shared by visitors outside GreenlitX's control (for example, a visitor's own screenshot) cannot be recalled by GreenlitX.

A3. Review is human and publication is not automatic

Every project submission is subject to review before anything appears publicly.

Submission does not guarantee:

  • Publication on GreenlitX;
  • Any particular audience response or level of pledge interest;
  • The opening of a funding campaign;
  • Financing, production, completion, or distribution; or
  • Employment, casting, or professional engagement for any person.

A4. Your declarations

By submitting a project, you confirm that:

  • The project is your original work, or you hold all rights needed to submit it and to grant the license in Section A2;
  • The submission does not infringe another person's copyright, trademark, privacy, publicity, or other rights;
  • Any real people, music, brands, artwork, footage, or existing works referenced or included are cleared by you to the extent clearance is required;
  • Any AI-generated material included in the submission is identified as such where the submission form asks, and the inclusion of AI-generated material does not by itself establish production activity; and
  • The information you provide — including development status and any proposed funding figure — is truthful and is not presented in a way that suggests the project is financed, cast, or in production when it is not.

You are responsible for what you submit.

A5. Funding is separate and conditional

Publication of a project does not open a payment campaign.

Pledge interest in a published project is governed by the Pledge and Funding Terms: it is currently nonbinding, no payment method is collected, and nothing is charged. A proposed funding target supplied in a submission is not a published campaign goal. A campaign goal, if one is ever set, is set per project by the owner of GreenlitX and published separately — it is never auto-derived from an estimated production budget or from a figure in a submission.

A6. Withdrawal

You may withdraw a project submission or a published project at any time by contacting GreenlitX at: GreenlitX — team@greenlitx.com

GreenlitX will remove a withdrawn published project from public view within [timeframe to be settled by legal review] of a verified request.

Pledge interest recorded for a withdrawn project will be treated as lapsed and will never be charged. Under any future payment model, pledges recorded but not yet charged for a withdrawn project would be cancelled and never charged.

A7. No submission fee at present

GreenlitX currently charges no submission or listing fee for project submissions.

Any future submission, listing, or platform fee must be disclosed in this Agreement and — where it would affect collected funds — in the Pledge and Funding Terms and Funding Transparency page before it is charged.

A8. No restriction on your other work

Nothing in this Agreement restricts you from pitching, developing, financing, or producing your project anywhere else, at any time, including while it is submitted to or published on GreenlitX, unless you sign a separate written agreement that expressly says otherwise.

Part B — Completed-film submissions for Watch Free

B1. Who may submit

Only a film's rights holder, or a person authorized by the rights holder to act for them, may submit a completed film for Watch Free. By submitting, you confirm that you are one of those people.

B2. You retain your intellectual property

You retain all rights in your completed film. GreenlitX claims no ownership of the film under this Agreement.

The only permission GreenlitX receives is the limited permission expressly stated in Section B3.

B3. The limited permission you grant

By submitting a completed film, you grant GreenlitX permission to:

  • List the film in the Watch Free library with the credits and details you supply; and
  • Present the film to visitors through an embed of the film's existing authorized copy on its source platform (for example, an official video-platform upload or an archive item you control).

GreenlitX does not, under this permission, download, copy, re-host, edit, or sell your film.

B4. The source copy stays in control

The embed keeps the source provider's player, controls, branding, and attribution.

Availability may change. If you remove, restrict, or region-limit the source copy, the GreenlitX presentation may stop working and the listing will be removed. GreenlitX does not guarantee that a Watch Free film will remain available for any period.

B5. Your declarations

By submitting a completed film, you confirm that:

  • The film is complete;
  • The credits and details you supply are accurate;
  • The source copy is lawfully available for free viewing and embedding in the territories where it is offered; and
  • The listing basis is accurately described — for example, public domain, an open license, or rights-holder authorization — and any license conditions will be followed.

B6. Revocation

You may revoke Watch Free permission at any time by contacting GreenlitX at: GreenlitX — team@greenlitx.com

GreenlitX will remove the listing within [timeframe to be settled by legal review — working draft in earlier platform material suggested 5 business days] of a verified request. Revocation is not retroactive and does not by itself create a claim over views that already occurred while the listing was authorized.

B7. No payment and no exclusivity

Watch Free listing is free and non-exclusive. GreenlitX pays no fee for a Watch Free listing under this Agreement and claims no rights in the film beyond Section B3.

A Watch Free film carries no pledge or fundraising feature unless a separate written agreement expressly provides otherwise.

Part C — General provisions

C1. Materials you submit through other forms

Professional-interest registrations and other platform forms are submissions of information for review only. Registering professional interest is not an application outcome, an offer of employment, a casting decision, or a promise of engagement. Any personal information in a submission is handled under the Privacy Policy.

C2. Removal by GreenlitX

GreenlitX may decline, remove, or restrict a submission or listing that:

  • Violates the Terms of Service or Community Guidelines;
  • Is the subject of a copyright notice handled under the Copyright and DMCA Policy;
  • Contains information GreenlitX reasonably believes to be false about rights, development status, financing, or production; or
  • Creates a legal or security risk for the platform.

Removal of a project does not transfer any rights in it to GreenlitX.

C3. No partnership or representation

Submitting material to GreenlitX does not create a partnership, agency, employment, joint-venture, or representation relationship between you and GreenlitX.

C4. Changes to this Agreement

Changes to this Agreement will be posted on this page. A change will not expand GreenlitX's rights in a project or film you have already submitted beyond the license or permission you granted under the version in force at submission, unless you agree to the expanded right in writing.

C5. Contact

For submission questions, withdrawals, or Watch Free revocations: GreenlitX — team@greenlitx.com
