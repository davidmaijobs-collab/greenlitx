3. Film projects are in development — production is not guaranteed

Film projects presented on GreenlitX are in development unless a project page expressly states a different, verified status.

Listing a project on GreenlitX does not mean that the project:

Audience follows and pledge interest do not guarantee production. A project may be changed, paused, withdrawn, or abandoned at any stage.

4. Estimated budgets are not fundraising goals

Some project presentations may refer to an estimated production budget. An estimated budget is an early planning figure for what a production might cost.

An estimated budget is not:

  • A fundraising or campaign goal;

  • A promise about how any future funds would be sufficient to complete a film.

Campaign goals, if any are set for a project in the future, are a separate figure. See the Funding Transparency page and the Pledge and Funding Terms.

Posters, images, concept art, teasers, and other presentation materials on GreenlitX may include AI-generated or otherwise conceptual material.

Concept materials illustrate an idea in development. They do not establish that:

  • Production activity has taken place;
  • Any person shown or named in concept material is attached to a project;
  • A location, set, cast, or crew exists for a project; or
  • A finished film will look or sound like the concept material.

Where a project page uses fictional billing or similar poster conventions as part of a concept presentation, that presentation must not be read as a statement of real cast or crew attachments.

6. Pledge interest — current status

At present, pledges on GreenlitX are nonbinding pledge interest only.

That means:

  • No card is charged;

  • Recording pledge interest is not a payment, deposit, purchase, donation, or binding payment commitment; and
  • Pledge interest will never be silently converted into a payment, authorization, subscription, or binding commitment.

Any future payment feature would require a separate legal and payment-readiness process and a separate, explicit consent flow before any charge could occur. The full current and intended pledge rules are in the Pledge and Funding Terms, which form part of these Terms.

Stripe is not active on GreenlitX. GreenlitX does not currently process payments through Stripe or any other payment processor for film pledges.

7. Supporters are not investors

Following a project, recording pledge interest, or — if payment features are ever activated — making a conditional pledge does not make you an investor.

GreenlitX offers no:

  • Dividends, interest, revenue share, profit participation, or other financial returns; or
  • Right to repayment or return based on a project's commercial performance.

Nothing on GreenlitX is an offer to sell securities or a solicitation of an investment. Do not record pledge interest or, in future, make a pledge in expectation of profit.
