How GreenlitX works

  1. 1. Discover

    Browse film projects from independent filmmakers across every genre — live-action, documentary, animation and more — and find the stories that deserve to exist.

  2. 2. Support

    Cast your vote for the projects you believe in and, in the full platform, make a pledge toward the ones you want to see produced.

  3. 3. Reach the threshold

    GreenlitX is built on conditional funding: supporters would only ever be charged if a project reaches its clearly stated funding threshold. Below the threshold, nobody pays anything.

  4. 4. Watch it get made

    Funded projects move into production with milestone updates, and supporters share the premiere when the film is finished.

Why audiences matter

Every year, remarkable scripts are turned down by a handful of executives while formula sequels are approved in minutes. GreenlitX exists to close that gap.

Stories studios overlook

Audiences know what moves them. GreenlitX lets that knowledge — not just market formulas — decide which stories get a chance.

A real signal, early

Votes and pledges show filmmakers genuine audience demand before a single scene is shot, reducing guesswork for everyone.

A voice in the culture

Supporting a film on GreenlitX means helping decide what the culture gets to watch — not just consuming what was decided for you.

Supporter participation & rewards

Supporters are the studio. These demonstration tiers show how participation might be recognised when the full platform launches. All amounts on this page are simulated — no money is collected in this prototype, and no financial returns are promised.

About acting opportunities: some tiers may include opportunities to apply for auditions or small roles when a production is casting. An acting opportunity is not guaranteed simply because someone pledges money. All casting participation depends on production requirements, eligibility, contractual terms and professional selection by the production team.

For actors & filmmakers

GreenlitX is being built as a meeting point for the people who make films and the audiences who champion them. No actor, studio or producer has endorsed GreenlitX — this page simply explains the opportunity the platform is designed to create.

For actors

  • Discover emerging projects at the development stage, before casting is locked.
  • Explore potential casting opportunities as funded projects move toward production.
  • Connect with promising filmmakers and follow the projects you believe in.
  • Build relationships with creative teams early, when collaborations are formed.

Casting is always a professional selection process. Being a GreenlitX supporter may surface opportunities to apply — it never guarantees a role. All participation depends on production requirements, eligibility and contractual terms.

For filmmakers

  • Showcase original projects with a cinematic project page.
  • Test audience interest with real votes and simulated pledges before you shoot.
  • Build a supporter community that follows your film from pitch to premiere.
  • Develop visibility before production, on your own terms.
  • Potentially attract future financing partners with demonstrated audience demand.
