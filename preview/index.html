How GreenlitX works

Discover. Browse original film projects in development — every filmmaking method is welcome: traditional live action, films made primarily with generative AI, hybrids of both, and animation.

Green Light. Give the films you believe in a Green Light. One per person per film — real votes, counted honestly, starting from zero.

Help make it happen. When founding memberships open in January 2027, supporters will be able to back films with conditional pledges: you are only charged if a project reaches its funding goal by its deadline.
