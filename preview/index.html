How GreenlitX works

Discover. Browse original film projects in development — every filmmaking method is welcome: traditional live action, films made primarily with generative AI, hybrids of both, and animation.

Green Light. Give the films you believe in a Green Light. One per person per film — real votes, counted honestly, starting from zero.

Help make it happen. Back the films you believe in with a conditional pledge — $5, $10, $25, $50, $100, or your own amount. Your card is charged once, and only if the project reaches 100% of its funding goal by its deadline. If it falls short, you are charged nothing.

For film professionals

GreenlitX films will need the people who make cinema. Tell the team who you are and what you do — when productions are staffed, registered professionals are the first to hear.

Registering interest is an expression of interest only — it is not a job offer, a casting call, or a promise of work, and nobody has been hired for any project yet. Your details are sent to the GreenlitX team only when you submit this form.
