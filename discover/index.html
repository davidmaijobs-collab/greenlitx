Submissions may be accepted, declined, or require additional information. Submission does not guarantee financing or production.

What We're Looking For

We're looking for original film and series projects from independent filmmakers around the world.

  • Feature films, shorts, documentaries, or series
  • Original concepts, scripts, or early-stage projects
  • Projects from independent creators and production teams
  • Compelling stories with a clear vision and audience potential

Important Information

  • Every submission is reviewed by a person.
  • Publication is not automatic.
  • Submission does not guarantee acceptance, financing, or production.
  • Filmmakers retain ownership of their projects.
  • Submitting grants only the review permissions described in the Submission Agreement.
