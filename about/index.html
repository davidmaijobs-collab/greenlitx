About GreenlitX

Hollywood decides what gets made today. On GreenlitX, the audience does.

We give original stories a public stage, and let audiences show what deserves to be made. A new, transparent and community-driven approach to filmmaking.

Our mission

Give original film projects a public stage and let audiences participate from the beginning — through open discovery, visible interest, and transparent engagement.

GreenlitX exists to give audiences a real say in which original film projects move forward — and to give those projects a public place to be discovered, followed, and supported before a traditional gatekeeper has decided their fate for them.

Our mission is simple: build a platform where interest is visible, participation is open, and the decision about what deserves to be made is informed by the people who would actually watch it.

Why GreenlitX exists

Too many original stories struggle to reach audiences because traditional decisions happen behind closed doors. GreenlitX gives creators a place to be discovered and audiences an earlier voice.

Most films never reach an audience for one reason: they never get past a closed decision. A small number of executives, committees, and buyers decide — in private, on criteria audiences never see — which stories are financed and which are set aside, often before a single future viewer has had the chance to respond to them.

That system leaves two gaps. Audiences have no meaningful way to signal, early enough to matter, what they want to see. And original projects without an existing connection into that system have no public way to demonstrate that an audience exists for them.

GreenlitX was created to close those gaps in the open: a catalog anyone can browse, projects anyone can follow, and interest that is recorded and visible rather than guessed at in a closed room.

Where GreenlitX is now — stated honestly

GreenlitX is an early-stage platform. The catalog of original projects is in concept development. Free accounts, following, recorded pledge interest, project submissions, and professional-interest registration are the live participation features. The Watch Free collection is live, within the lawful pool available for it. Payments are not yet active. No pledges are charged, no paid memberships are sold, and no money is collected through the platform today. A founding membership campaign is planned — not yet open — and proposed paid tiers are under consideration, not yet finalized (see the Membership page).

Transparency and Important Notes

  • Film-specific pledge interest is nonbinding.
  • Recording pledge interest does not charge a payment method.
  • Memberships are separate from individual film pledges.
  • Film submissions are reviewed and do not guarantee acceptance or publication.
  • Audience interest does not guarantee financing, production, distribution, or release.
  • Project creators retain their rights subject to the applicable submission terms.
